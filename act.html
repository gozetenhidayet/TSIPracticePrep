Practice by skill, not by guesswork

ScorePath uses original practice questions aligned to the skills students are expected to practice. Timed mode protects simulation integrity; untimed mode adds explanations, notes, highlighting and targeted review.

Use results immediately

After a session, review misses, timing patterns and skill accuracy. The most useful next step is a short targeted set on the weakest current skill, followed by spaced review.

Student workflow

  1. Choose a test or diagnostic.
  2. Complete questions without distractions.
  3. Review why answers were right or wrong.
  4. Practice a similar skill.
  5. Track progress over time.
