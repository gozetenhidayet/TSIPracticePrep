What to practice for TSI Math

Build confidence across quantitative reasoning, algebraic reasoning, geometric and spatial reasoning, and probabilistic and statistical reasoning. Use mixed practice to identify weak areas, then move into focused skill sets.

How to use this practice

Start untimed when you are learning a skill. Review each explanation and the reason each answer choice works or fails. Use timed practice later when you want to work on pacing and decision-making.

Practice with explanations

After answering, use the review tools to study the solution path rather than memorizing the letter of the correct answer. Focused repetition should target the skill that caused the miss.
