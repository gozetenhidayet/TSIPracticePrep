Found a mistake? Have a question?

TSI Practice Prep is corrected quickly when something is wrong. Send the question ID, a bug, or anything else below — there's no email address to copy or scrape here, just this form.

Send us a message

Use this for anything: a possible error in a practice question (include the question ID shown above the prompt, for example SAT-V17-M-ALG-104), a Teacher Classroom question, a partnership note, or general feedback. Messages are routed privately to the TSI Practice Prep team.

Site owner note: this form delivers to the site owner's inbox via FormSubmit.co (no account or API key needed). The very first message sent from this form triggers a one-time confirmation email from FormSubmit to that inbox — it must be opened and confirmed once before messages start arriving; every message after that is delivered automatically.

Official exam questions

TSI Practice Prep cannot help with official test registration, accommodations, scores, or technical issues on test day — those go through College Board, ACT, or the relevant Texas education agency directly. See Resources for the right official contact in each case.
