Corrections & feedback

Help us identify a question or accessibility issue.

The most useful report includes the exact question ID, what appears incorrect, and the reasoning or source that supports the correction.

Question correction report

Before publishing the production site, connect this page to your preferred support form or helpdesk. When reporting an academic issue, include:

  • Question ID
  • Exam and section
  • The answer you believe is correct
  • Why the current key or explanation appears wrong
  • Any official source relevant to a format/policy issue

Accessibility feedback

Include the device/browser, the feature you were using, and the exact barrier (keyboard navigation, screen reader label, contrast, zoom, touch target, timer, or another issue).
