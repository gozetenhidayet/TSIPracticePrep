Read for evidence

Practice central ideas, evidence, inference, vocabulary in context, text purpose, and relationships between ideas. Strong answers should be supported by the text rather than outside assumptions.

Practice expression and conventions

Review transitions, rhetorical goals, sentence boundaries, agreement, punctuation, and other conventions that affect clarity and effectiveness.

Study the wrong choices

After a miss, identify what made the distractor tempting. Common traps include answers that are too broad, unsupported, opposite to the text, or grammatically possible but rhetorically ineffective.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SAT Reading and Writing practice free?

Yes. The practice tools can be started without a paid subscription.

What skills are included?

Practice includes comprehension, evidence, vocabulary in context, transitions, rhetorical goals, grammar, and conventions.

Can I review explanations?

Yes. Practice review is designed to help you understand the reasoning behind the correct answer and the distractors.
