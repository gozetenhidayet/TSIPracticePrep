A better weekly study cycle

1. Diagnose

Start with a mixed diagnostic to identify the two skills that need the most attention.

2. Learn the mistake

Read the explanation and the distractor reasoning. Classify the mistake: concept, process, reading, or pacing.

3. Re-practice

Build a focused set with fresh questions on the weak skill. Increase difficulty only when accuracy stabilizes.

4. Simulate

Use a full timed simulation periodically to test pacing and endurance—not as the only form of study.
