Core reading skills

Practice central ideas, supporting details, inference, vocabulary in context, author purpose, text structure, and relationships between claims and evidence.

Use evidence from the text

When two choices seem possible, return to the exact wording of the passage. The best answer should be supported by the text and should match the scope of the question.

Build accuracy before speed

Start untimed and focus on evidence. Once your accuracy becomes more consistent, use paced practice to make your reading decisions more efficient.
