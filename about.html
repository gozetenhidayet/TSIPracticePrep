About ScorePath

Independent practice built around understanding.

ScorePath Practice is designed to help students practice SAT, ACT, and TSIA2 skills, understand mistakes, and decide what to study next—without pretending that practice results are official scores.

What we are building

A free, browser-based test-prep platform with diagnostics, question-bank practice, timed simulations, mistake review, writing tools, progress tracking, and teacher assignment building.

What we do not claim

We do not claim affiliation with College Board, ACT, Inc., or Texas state agencies. We do not label independent practice questions as official questions, and we do not guarantee a score, admission decision, placement result, scholarship, or graduation outcome.

Our learning philosophy

Practice is most useful when students can explain why an answer is correct, why another choice is wrong, and what pattern caused the mistake. ScorePath therefore emphasizes explanation quality, distractor analysis, focused re-practice, and progress by skill.
