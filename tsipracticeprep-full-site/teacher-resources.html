What the Teacher Classroom does

Every student in your class or tutoring group joins the same live room with a short class code plus their first name, last initial, and grade — no student email or password required. From there you control the session and see results as they come in.

Live room controlStart, pause, lock, or end a practice session for the whole room at once, so everyone moves through the same block of practice together.
Question & skill analyticsSee accuracy broken down by individual question and by skill (e.g. TSIA2 Algebraic Reasoning, SAT Craft & Structure) as your class practices, not just a final score.
Student progress historyTrack how each joined student is doing across a session, so you can spot who needs a follow-up conversation before the bell rings, not after.
Same-question or randomized assignmentsAssign every student the identical set for direct comparison, or randomize per student to reduce over-the-shoulder copying during in-class practice.

Who this is for

Classroom teachers running a shared TSIA2, SAT, or ACT practice block, tutors managing a small group, and test-prep programs that want live visibility into who is struggling with what — without asking students to create their own accounts or install anything.

How to get started

  1. Create a teacher account and open the Teacher Classroom.
  2. Generate a class code and share it with your students, in person or over a shared screen.
  3. Students join with the class code plus first name, last initial, and grade — done, no student sign-up flow.
  4. Start a session, choose TSIA2, SAT, or ACT, and watch live accuracy and pacing as your class works.
TSI Practice Prep's Teacher Classroom is a free practice and analytics tool, not a gradebook or LMS replacement — it's built for the specific job of running a shared, live practice session and seeing where a class actually struggles.
