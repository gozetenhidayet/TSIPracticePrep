What to practice

Linear equations, systems and inequalities.

Use a short untimed set first if the skill is new or accuracy is low. Move to timed work after the reasoning is reliable. Review each miss by identifying the concept, the tempting distractor and the step that should change next time.

Efficient study loop

  1. Answer without looking at the explanation.
  2. Classify each miss: concept, reading trap, process or pacing.
  3. Solve a fresh similar question.
  4. Return later through spaced review.
