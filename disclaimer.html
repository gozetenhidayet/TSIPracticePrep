Independent test-prep disclaimer

ScorePath Practice is designed for learning. It is not an official testing platform.

Independent status

ScorePath Practice is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by College Board, ACT, Inc., or the Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board. References to SAT, ACT, and TSIA2 identify the exams students are preparing for.

Original practice questions

Unless specifically identified as an external official resource, questions presented inside ScorePath are independent practice questions. Students seeking official sample questions should use the testing organization’s official practice resources.

Practice analytics

Accuracy percentages, skill mastery, pacing observations, and next-step recommendations are educational analytics based on practice activity. They are not official scores or validated score predictions.

Requirements can vary

Admission, graduation, placement, college-readiness, dual-credit, scholarship, and testing requirements can vary by state, school, college, cohort, and program. Verify the requirement that applies to you.
