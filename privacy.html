Privacy

Privacy designed for a practice-first experience.

This standalone version stores most practice activity in the student’s current browser rather than requiring an account.

Information stored in this standalone version

The site may use browser local storage for completed-question counts, correct-answer counts, mistake lists, bookmarks, practice history, saved writing drafts, study-goal preferences, recent-question history, and self-selected mistake reasons. This data remains associated with the browser profile unless the site is later connected to a server-side account system.

Advertising and analytics

If advertising or analytics services are activated on the production site, those providers may use cookies, device information, or similar technologies according to their own policies and the consent requirements that apply to the visitor. ScorePath should publish the actual vendors used before enabling them.

Children and school use

Schools and families should evaluate whether a production deployment meets the privacy, parental-consent, student-data, and district requirements that apply to their students. The standalone file does not create student accounts or transmit progress to a ScorePath server.

Clearing local progress

Users can remove locally stored practice data through their browser’s site-data/storage controls. A future production account system should provide its own data-access and deletion controls.

Policy updates

This policy should be updated whenever the production site adds accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, third-party forms, payment features, or other data collection.
