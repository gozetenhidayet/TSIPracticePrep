Most of this site never leaves your browser.

Practice without an account and your answers, mistakes, and progress stay only on this device. Creating an optional account or joining a live classroom shares a little more — this page says exactly what.

Practicing without an account

  • No account, email, or password is required to practice.
  • Your answers, accuracy, bookmarks, mistake list, and pacing history are stored only in this browser's local storage, on this device.
  • Nothing about your practice history is sent to ScorePath or to anyone else while practicing without an account.

Optional student account

Creating a free account (or "Continue on This Device Only") is optional and is only used to keep your progress available across devices. When cloud sync is configured by the site owner, the email address, encrypted password (handled entirely by Firebase Authentication, never seen in plain text by ScorePath), first name, and grade you enter are stored to enable that sync. Choosing "Continue on This Device Only" instead stores your first name and grade locally, with no password and no account created anywhere.

Teacher Classroom / live sessions

Joining a class code shares only your first name, last initial, and grade with your teacher's session, plus your in-session answers, timing, and any focus/fullscreen warnings if your teacher has enabled Secure Timed Mode. In the default local-demo mode this data is relayed directly between browser tabs on your device and is not stored on a server. If the site owner has configured cloud sync, the same session data instead relays through that sync provider for the duration of the session.

Ads & cookies

  • ScorePath Practice can optionally show ads served by Google AdSense to help keep practice free. Ads are off by default and only turn on once the site owner configures a real AdSense account.
  • When ads are on, Google and its advertising partners may set cookies or similar technology in your browser to show ads and measure how they perform. This is separate from, and never affects, your local practice history or account data.
  • You choose whether to accept these cookies the first time you visit, and you can change your mind anytime with the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising directly through Google at adssettings.google.com, or through the industry-wide opt-out at optout.aboutads.info.
  • ScorePath Practice does not knowingly show personalized ads to children, consistent with Google AdSense's policies for sites whose audience may include users under 13.

What ScorePath never does

ScorePath Practice does not sell practice data, does not share individual student responses with advertisers, and does not report practice scores to any school, college, or testing organization.
