Content quality

Editorial & Question Quality Standards

A serious practice platform needs more than many questions. It needs a repeatable process for checking answer keys, explanations, distractors, tested skills, and time-sensitive exam information.

Quality checklistOriginal practiceOfficial structure sourcesQuestion reviewCorrectionsClaims & estimates

Original practice content

Practice questions should be independently written to train the same academic skills without copying protected official test banks. Any official sample material linked by ScorePath remains the property of its source and should be accessed at the official source.

Official structure sources

Digital SAT structure and official SAT resources are checked against College Board. ACT section structure and optional components are checked against ACT. TSIA2 structure and Texas college-readiness information are checked against College Board ACCUPLACER and Texas higher-education sources.

Question review standard

Required fields

Unique ID, exam, section, skill, difficulty, prompt, answer choices, valid correct-answer index, explanation, choice-by-choice analysis, and solution strategy.

Academic check

The correct answer must be defensible from the prompt, data, passage, grammar rule, or mathematical reasoning. Distractors should be plausible but demonstrably incorrect.

Language check

Prompts should avoid unnecessary ambiguity, hidden assumptions, misleading wording, and accidental clues to the answer.

Technical check

The live bank runs a structural integrity check to flag duplicate IDs, invalid answer indexes, duplicate answer choices, and incomplete explanations.

Corrections policy

When a question is found to be ambiguous, miskeyed, factually inaccurate, or poorly explained, it should be removed from active practice until corrected and re-reviewed. Content updates should preserve a clear distinction between independent practice and official exam content.

Score and outcome claims

No score guarantees. Practice percentages, mastery levels, and recommendations are learning signals. They are not official test scores and do not guarantee admissions, placement, scholarships, graduation, or other outcomes.

Question integrity: no lightly rewritten live-test items

ScorePath Practice does not use recalled, leaked, copied, or lightly rewritten SAT, ACT, or TSIA2 test questions. Practice items are independently written and then restricted to the current official content blueprint. Official released practice materials are used to understand tested skills, formats, and difficulty expectations—not as text to copy.

Blueprint-only content

Active questions are filtered so that SAT items remain within current Digital SAT Reading & Writing and Math domains, ACT items remain within current Enhanced ACT reporting categories, and TSIA2 items remain within official Mathematics and ELAR content categories. Items that cannot be mapped to a tested domain are excluded from the active bank.
