Are these official TSIA2, SAT, or ACT questions?

No. TSI Practice Prep uses independently written practice questions aligned to tested skills.

Is the score estimate official?

No. It is a practice estimate based on this TSI Practice Prep set. Official scores use the testing organization's scoring process.

Does a college match mean I will be admitted?

No. The list only shows schools whose reported score ranges are near your estimate. Admissions consider many other factors.
