What the TSIA2 is actually for.

This page explains the general purpose of the TSIA2 in Texas higher education. It is not a substitute for your school's or college's official guidance, and rules can change — always confirm current requirements with the sources linked below.

What the TSIA2 generally determines

The Texas Success Initiative Assessment 2.0 (TSIA2) is used by Texas public colleges and universities to help determine college readiness in Mathematics and English Language Arts and Reading (ELAR), and to help place students into the appropriate credit-bearing, corequisite, or developmental coursework. Some students are exempt from TSIA2 testing entirely based on prior SAT/ACT scores, class rank, or other criteria set by the state and by individual institutions.

Why this page stays general

Exact exemption thresholds, passing standards, dual-credit eligibility rules, and how a given score maps to placement can vary by institution and change from year to year. Rather than state specific numbers here that could go stale or vary by school, ScorePath Practice points to the bodies that actually set and maintain those rules.
