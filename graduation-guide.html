Graduation and testing requirements: verify the rule that applies to you.

Graduation, assessment, college-readiness, and dual-credit requirements can differ by state, district, school, graduating cohort, and program. Use this page as a verification checklist—not as a substitute for your counselor or official state rules.

What to verify

Graduation credits

Confirm required credits and course categories for your graduating cohort.

State assessments

Confirm whether passing scores, participation, alternatives, exemptions, or end-of-course exams apply.

SAT / ACT use

Check whether your state or district provides a school-day exam and how scores may be used for accountability, admissions, scholarships, or placement.

College readiness / dual credit

Texas students should verify TSIA2 exemptions, college-readiness standards, and dual-credit rules with the college and Texas sources.

Never rely on an old screenshot

Requirements change. Always check an official state education agency, your district/school, your counselor, and—when college placement or dual credit is involved—the college itself.

Planning checklist

  • Identify your graduating cohort/year.
  • Print or bookmark your district’s graduation plan.
  • Verify current testing requirements and important dates.
  • Confirm accommodations or alternative testing rules early.
  • For college applications, verify each college’s current test policy.
  • For Texas college readiness, verify TSIA2 requirements and exemptions with the receiving institution.
